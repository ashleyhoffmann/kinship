# Water v1 → v2 coverage

Water v2 (October 2026) turns the six v1 units into six steps, each an ability, with a calculator whose numbers carry forward. Most v1 section text is reused word for word: `water.js` keeps the v1 `units()` content and the new steps pull sections from it with `OLD(unit, section)`. The full v1 file is kept beside this note as `water-v1.js` (not loaded by the site).

Old links still work: `resolve()` in `water.js` maps v1 routes (`uses`, `storage`, `testing`, `costs`, `cows` and their sections) to the new ones.
